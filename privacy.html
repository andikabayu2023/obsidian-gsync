Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Purpose

This website describes a personal Obsidian GSync configuration. It is not a separate synchronization service operated by the website owner.

Google Drive Access

The Obsidian GSync plugin uses Google OAuth authorization to access Google Drive for the synchronization of files selected by the user.

Data Handling

Files selected for synchronization are transferred between the user's Obsidian installation and Google Drive through the plugin and Google's services.

Third-party Services

Google Drive and the Obsidian GSync plugin are third-party services. Their respective privacy policies and terms also apply.

Contact

For questions about this personal configuration, contact the email address listed on the OAuth consent screen.
